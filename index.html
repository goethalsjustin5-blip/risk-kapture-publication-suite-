03 / FIRST VERTICAL SLICE

Prove documentation quality before expanding automation.

  1. Property and policy intake

    Create the structured pre-loss record.

  2. Room-by-room evidence capture

    Collect photos, video, contents, metadata, and location.

  3. Immutable evidence upload

    Record hashes, versions, timestamps, and audit events.

  4. Human-reviewed scope and estimate

    Use documented pricing sources and versioned overrides.

  5. Claim-package generation

    Produce structured, exportable, review-ready documentation.

  6. Read-only carrier review

    Test completeness, usability, and payment confidence.

PILOT PRINCIPLE

Do not begin by promising autonomous damage reconstruction. Begin by proving that Risk Kapture materially improves evidence quality, claim-package completeness, and review speed.

04 / WHY NOW

The infrastructure now exists.

  • Modern smartphones can capture high-quality spatial and visual evidence.
  • Public hazard and geospatial datasets can establish a low-cost baseline.
  • Carriers face persistent pressure to improve claim speed and customer trust.
  • Municipalities need reusable records before federal recovery begins.
  • AI can support triage and comparison while humans retain review authority.
05 / WHY RISK KAPTURE

The moat is the record, not the estimate.

  • Pre-loss evidence becomes more valuable at the moment of loss.
  • One record supports policyholder, carrier, contractor, and civic workflows.
  • Each completed event improves operational intelligence and readiness scoring.
  • White-label distribution can embed the platform inside trusted local brands.
  • Workflow, auditability, and interoperability are harder to replicate than a single model.
