01 / THE ASK

A $2.0 million pre-seed round.

The capital target is sized to reach a pilot-ready product, complete controlled paid deployments, establish security and evidence controls, support enterprise sales cycles, and reach monthly operating break-even in the base case.

$2.0M

Recommended capital raise

Target structure: early-stage equity or SAFE financing, subject to company formation, cap-table review, securities counsel, investor terms, and board approval. The model assumes no debt service.

Minimum viable first close: $750,000

Funds approximately nine to ten months of product hardening, pilot preparation, legal architecture, and first customer deployment. A first close alone does not fund the full path to profitability.

What the round must accomplish

  • Production-grade Home capture, evidence storage, review, and claim-package workflow.
  • Read-only Carrier console and Civic asset-readiness pilot.
  • Security architecture, privacy controls, contracts, insurance, and auditability.
  • At least four paid deployments or equivalent contracted revenue by Month 24.
  • Base-case Year 2 recognized revenue of $1.25 million and a credible path to Month 27 break-even.
Why not $150,000–$300,000?

That range can fund a narrow MVP and customer discovery. It cannot responsibly fund enterprise security, municipal implementation, carrier integration work, legal review, field operations, and a long sales cycle through profitability.

Definition of profitability

“Break-even” means positive monthly operating EBITDA before interest, taxes, depreciation, stock compensation, and unusual legal or financing costs. GAAP net income and positive free cash flow may occur later.

Compensation basis

Staffing assumptions are deliberately below a fully built enterprise team and use blended contracting. National BLS benchmarks show May 2025 mean annual wages of approximately $148,100 for software developers, $132,510 for information security analysts, $126,800 for data scientists, and $110,740 for project management specialists. Private-industry benefits represented 30.1% of employer compensation in March 2026, which is why fully loaded hiring materially exceeds salary alone.

The raise is released against measurable de-risking.

MONTH 6

Mobile capture, evidence upload, human-reviewed scope, claim-package export, audit events, and controlled administrator review.

MONTH 9

First paid deployment

Paid municipal, partner, or carrier-adjacent pilot with baseline metrics, implementation SOPs, consent, and support process.

MONTH 12

MONTH 24–27

Base-case Year 2 revenue of $1.25 million, accelerating contracted backlog, and monthly operating EBITDA turning positive in Month 27.

06 / CORE ASSUMPTIONS

What must be validated before the numbers become a forecast.

Municipal economics

  • Initial paid pilot/implementation: approximately $125,000.
  • Recurring municipal platform: approximately $60,000–$110,000 annually by scope and population.
  • Procurement cycle: six to twelve months unless using a qualified partner or existing vehicle.

Carrier economics

  • Discovery or controlled pilot: approximately $250,000–$400,000.
  • Production white-label platform: approximately $500,000–$1.1 million annually with implementation and usage.
  • Sales and security review: twelve to twenty-four months without an incumbent channel partner.

Partner SaaS and packages

  • Partner/contractor workspace begins with the existing directional assumption of $99 per month.
  • Base planning should use 2%–5% free-to-paid conversion and 5%–8% monthly churn until real cohorts exist.
  • Completed package fee assumption: approximately $75–$125, preferably sponsored by a carrier, municipality, or partner.

Gross margin and cloud

  • Gross margin expands from 60% in Year 1 to 81% in Year 5 as implementation becomes standardized.
  • Cloud is modeled as usage-based rather than a fixed enterprise commitment.
  • Evidence-heavy storage, retrieval, mapping, support, and human review remain direct costs and must be measured per property and claim.
07 / FINANCIAL RISKS

The downside case requires more capital.

Downside outcome

If municipal procurement, carrier security review, and paid pilot conversion take longer than modeled, monthly operating break-even moves to approximately Month 42. Under that scenario, the initial $2.0 million raise is not sufficient; the company would likely need an additional $1.0–$1.2 million or equivalent non-dilutive and customer-funded capital.

  • Customer concentration during the first three years.
  • Slow public procurement and reimbursement timing.
  • Carrier integration and security requirements expanding scope.
  • High field-support cost if onboarding is not self-service.
  • Evidence storage, media processing, and human-review costs exceeding assumptions.
  • Unauthorized-public-adjusting, privacy, insurance, procurement, or data-rights issues delaying commercialization.
